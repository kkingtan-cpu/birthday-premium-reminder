How to use this every Monday
  1. Download your latest policy listing as PDF (all pages), or export a customer list as Excel or CSV.
  2. Open this page and load the files in step 1. Pick all the PDF pages at once.
  3. Look at step 2. Anything marked "Left out" wasn't used. Fix it in the source if needed.
  4. In step 3, edit a message if you like, then tap Copy and paste it into WhatsApp, SMS or email. If the list has phone numbers, WhatsApp… opens the chat with the message ready; you still press Send yourself.
  5. Tap Mark sent so you know it's done. Tap Download PDF for the week's list with greeting cards and messages.
