# Birthday & Premium Reminder: how to use it

**What it fixes:** you'll no longer forget customer birthdays or overdue premiums. Every Monday the app lists who needs a message this week and writes the message for you in English and Chinese (中文).

**App link (private to you, works on phone and PC):** https://claude.ai/artifact/FUjRb221CMVvRjmKczVqAN

## What you need each week
- Your **Policy Listing** from the agent portal, saved as PDF. Save **all** pages: today that was two files, "page 1" (500 records) and "page 2" (292 records).
- Nothing else. No logins or connections are needed. The PDF already has the policy holder's DOB, the premium due date, the amount and the status.

## Every Monday (about 5 minutes)
1. Open the app link. You'll get a reminder in the Claude app at 8:30am on Mondays.
2. **Step 1:** tap **Choose files** in "Customer or policy list" and pick **both** PDF files together.
3. **Step 2:** read the notes. "Left out" means a row couldn't be read safely and wasn't used; nothing is guessed.
4. **Step 3:** go through the three tabs:
   - **Birthdays:** customers whose birthday falls in the next 10 days. "Send on" shows the day to send, 3 days before the birthday.
   - **Premiums due:** in-force policies due soon. A customer with several policies due on the same day gets one message with the total.
   - **Overdue:** in-force policies past their due date, newest first. Items more than 90 days overdue are hidden; you can change that in Settings.
5. Each card has an **English / 中文 / Both 两种** switch. Edit the message if you like, then tap **WhatsApp…**. Type the customer's number once and the app remembers it on this phone; or leave it empty and choose the chat inside WhatsApp. WhatsApp opens with the message ready, and you press Send. You can also tap **Copy** and paste. In the PDF the Chinese text is shown as a picture, so copy Chinese messages from the app. Your listing has no phone numbers, so the WhatsApp button stays off. Tap **Save card image** to attach the birthday card.
6. Tap **Mark sent** when done. Tap **Download PDF** to save the whole week's list with cards and messages.

## Safety rules built in
- Your files are read only on your device. Nothing is uploaded or stored.
- The app never sends anything. You always press Send yourself.
- Only **Inforce** policies get premium reminders. Lapsed, terminated, withdrawn, fully paid and premium-waived policies are skipped and counted in step 2.
- No birthday wish goes to a customer who has no policy in force, or whose policy record shows they have died. If a family member's policy ended because of a death, the card shows a "please check" note.
- The listing is "as at" a date. Always check the latest payment in the portal before sending an overdue reminder.

## Sending the greeting together with the card
WhatsApp links can only carry text, so for a birthday the WhatsApp… window works in steps:
1. Tap **① 保存卡片 Save card** to save the card to your phone (or **复制卡片 Copy card**, if your phone allows it).
2. Tap **② Open WhatsApp** and send the greeting.
3. In the same chat, tap 📎 → Gallery and send the card.
If your phone allows sharing, a **Share card + message** button appears and sends both in one go.

## Birthday card styles
There are four styles: 金色典雅 Elegant gold (the default), 红色喜庆 Chinese red, 花卉 Floral and 气球 Balloons. Set your usual style in Settings, or tap a style under a card to change just that customer. The red style shows 福 for customers under 55 and 寿 for 55 and above. Cards for 55 and above also say 福如东海 · 寿比南山; younger customers get 身体健康 · 万事如意.

## Settings you can change
Sign-off name (filled in from the Agent Name in your listing), your company name, how many days ahead to remind (3), the language (English, 中文 or both), and the overdue limit (90 days).
